## Recurring devices
- **Fuse**: the burning cord along the top edge at every chapter start, labelled "Chapter N · title"; it burns 1/12 further each time.
- **Date-line bar**: italic event name over a rule, odometer year, about every 30 s.
- **The Heavenly Calendar (spine)**: 14 year-tiles, 1851–1864. Appears at Jintian; a tile is stamped red at each new year in the story; cracks run through it after the 1856 purge; the 14th tile shatters when the wall comes down; the whole strip burns on "the Heavenly Kingdom was gone".
- **Portrait medallions**: drawn from the same character art; a death greys the medallion with a red slash. No gore anywhere: massacres are crimson-tinted silhouettes, smoke, cuts to black.
- **Planted object and payoff: the pass list.** Planted 1:09 (Canton exam wall, Hong's empty slot, red REMEMBER tag, three-note motif). Echo 1, 5:04: the Taiping exam list with the Heavenly King's seal – "the man who had failed the exams now wrote them" (motif). Echo 2, 5:32: a Qing list with Zeng Guofan's column gilded (motif). Payoff 11:15 (90%): the same wall and list, then ABOLISHED 1905; the dynasty overthrown six years later; "outlived him by just forty-one years".
- **Callback plates**: Nanjing at dusk under siege (cold open = ch5 = ch10 = ch12), the throne hall (cold open, ch5, ch7, ch8, ch10), the Canton exam wall (ch1, ch2, ch11), the dream sky (ch1, ch2, ch12), the Qing throne room (ch2, ch11), the starving city at night (cold open = ch10).

## Accuracy notes (for the description or a pinned comment)
See taiping/plan_and_facts.md for the full fact sheet with sources. Additions made during production: Zeng Guofan passed the metropolitan (jinshi) examination in 1838; the Convention of Peking, 1860; Gordon took command in March 1863; Li Xiucheng was executed on 7 August 1864 and Hong Tianguifu at Nanchang in November 1864; Shi Dakai was put to death in June 1863 after surrendering at the Dadu River; First World War deaths are usually estimated at about 15–22 million.

## Known limits of this cut
- Runtime is 12:30, about 30 s over the 12-minute target, because the approved script was voiced unchanged at the house speed.
- Chinese text on screen is illustrative brush marks (no CJK font in the engine), by design.
- Names are pronounced through an IPA lexicon; they're approximations of Mandarin for an English narrator.
- The Taiping territory on the maps is an approximate outline of the lower-Yangtze heartland (about 1853–60), not a surveyed border.
